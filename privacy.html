Privacy & analytics

This website is hosted with GitHub Pages. GitHub may collect technical information, including IP addresses, to operate its hosting service. See GitHub’s privacy statement.

Owner analytics

When Statcounter is configured, it may receive your IP address, approximate location, browser and device information, page visits, referral source, visit duration, and return-visit information. It may use cookies to distinguish visitors. Reports are available only to the website owner and are not published on this website.

These reports help the owner understand readership, visit counts, timing, and traffic sources. An IP address does not reliably identify an individual, employer, or exact location. Network sharing and VPNs can affect these details.

Privacy signals

The site respects supported Global Privacy Control and Do Not Track signals. Browser privacy tools and content blockers may also prevent analytics requests. Previously collected records are governed by the analytics provider’s retention settings.
